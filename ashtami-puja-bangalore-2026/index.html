Ashtami Puja in Bangalore 2026: plan your BSS visit

The confirmed BSS ritual, Bhog and cultural programme for two Ashtami celebration days at The Occasion, Hoodi.

Quick answer

BSS has published two Ashtami programme days in 2026: Sunday, 18 October and Monday, 19 October. On both days, Ashtami Puja is at 6:00 AM, Pushpanjali is at 11:00 AM, and Bhog is served from 1:30 PM to 3:30 PM. Sandhi Puja is listed at 7:26 AM on 19 October.

Which Ashtami day should you choose?

For DandiyaChoose 18 October. The evening programme lists Karusanskriti at 6:30 PM, Sandhya Arati at 7:00 PM and DJ & Dandiya at 7:30 PM.
For Sandhi PujaChoose 19 October. The current BSS timetable lists Sandhi Puja at 7:26 AM.
For DhunuchiChoose 19 October. Dhaker Tale and the Dhunuchi Competition are scheduled at 7:30 PM after Sandhya Arati.
For Pushpanjali and BhogBoth days list Pushpanjali at 11:00 AM and Bhog from 1:30 PM to 3:30 PM.
