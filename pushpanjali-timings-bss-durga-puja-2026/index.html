What is Pushpanjali?

Pushpanjali is an offering of flowers made with prayer during Durga Puja. For many visitors, it is one of the central devotional moments of the morning programme.

This page gives the times announced by BSS; it does not prescribe ritual practice. The organising team may guide visitors at the venue. The current published information does not say whether flowers will be provided, whether visitors should bring their own, or whether a token or registration process will apply.

Which day should you choose?

For the Ashtami programme: BSS lists Pushpanjali at 11:00 AM on both 18 and 19 October. The Ashtami visitor guide explains the two days and the confirmed Sandhi Puja timing.

For Navami and Sylheti culture: On 20 October, Pushpanjali at 11:00 AM is followed by Dhamail of Unity at 12:00 PM and Bhog from 1:30 PM.

For Bhog: The confirmed serving window is 1:30–3:30 PM daily. See the BSS Bhog visitor guide for all six dates and the information that remains unconfirmed.
