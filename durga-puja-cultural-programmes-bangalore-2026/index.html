BSS Durga Puja cultural programmes in Bangalore 2026

Music, dance, community performances and traditional celebrations across six Puja days at The Occasion, Hoodi.

Quick answer

BSS Durga Puja 2026 runs from 16 to 21 October at The Occasion, Hoodi. The confirmed cultural programme includes Agomoni, children’s performances, a live band, Karusanskriti, Dandiya, Dhunuchi, Dhamail, Sindhur Khela and Shanti Jol. Bhog is served daily from 1:30 PM to 3:30 PM.

Cultural traditions at BSS

BSS is Bengaluru Sreehatta Sammelani, a socio-cultural association bringing together Sylhetis and friends of the community in Bengaluru. Everyone is welcome at the Puja. The programme combines Bengali Durga Puja traditions with Sylheti community culture.

If you want more context before visiting, read the BSS guides to Dhamail, Dhunuchi Naach and Sindoor Khela. First-time visitors can also use the first Durga Puja guide.

Plan the full visit

The cultural programme is one part of the day. BSS also has confirmed Puja and Pushpanjali timings, while Bhog is served from 1:30 PM to 3:30 PM daily. Check the schedule before travelling so you can plan the rituals, Bhog and evening programme together.

Frequently asked questions

When are the BSS Durga Puja cultural programmes?

Confirmed cultural programmes are scheduled from 16 to 21 October 2026 at The Occasion, Hoodi.

When are Dhamail and Dhunuchi?

Dhaker Tale and the Dhunuchi Competition are scheduled at 7:30 PM on 19 October. Dhamail of Unity is scheduled at 12:00 PM on 20 October.

When is Dandiya?

DJ & Dandiya are scheduled at 7:30 PM on 18 October, with another Dandiya programme at 7:30 PM on 20 October. See the dedicated Dandiya guide.

What time is Bhog served?

Bhog is served from 1:30 PM to 3:30 PM every Puja day from 16 to 21 October.

Where is BSS Durga Puja 2026?

The venue is The Occasion, Hoodi, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560048. Visitors from Hoodi, Whitefield and East Bengaluru can also use the local visitor guide.

Can non-Sylhetis attend?

Yes. Although BSS is a Sylheti association, everyone is welcome at the Durga Puja.
