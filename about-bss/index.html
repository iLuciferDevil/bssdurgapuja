About Bengaluru Sreehatta Sammelani

Bengaluru Sreehatta Sammelani, commonly known as BSS, is a socio-cultural association that brings together Sylhetis and friends of the community in Bengaluru. BSS was founded in 2005 and organises cultural and community activities through the year.

BSS Durga Puja

BSS celebrated its first Durgotsav in 2019. In 2026, BSS Durga Puja is being held from 16–21 October at The Occasion, Hoodi, Bengaluru.
