WHY BSS

A little bit of Sylhet, right here in Bengaluru.

BSS is more than an event. It is a community coming together to celebrate Maa Durga, our culture and the feeling of home.

From traditional rituals to Dhamail, cultural performances, adda and food, BSS Durga Puja brings together families, children, youth and elders every year.
