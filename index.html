Where is BSS Durga Puja 2026?

The Occasion Bengaluru, Sri Devi Anugraha, Anup Layout, Basi Reddy Garden, KR Puram Road, Hoodi, Bengaluru 560048.

When is the Puja?

The main BSS Durga Puja runs from 16 to 20 October 2026. Mahalaya is on 10 October and Maha Panchami is on 15 October.
