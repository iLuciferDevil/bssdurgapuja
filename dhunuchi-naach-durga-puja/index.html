What is Dhunuchi Naach?

Dhunuchi Naach is a devotional dance associated with Durga Puja, commonly performed around aarti. A dhunuchi is a traditional incense burner, often made from earthenware. The burning material inside produces fragrant smoke while dancers move to the rhythm of the dhaak, Bengal’s festival drum.

The movement can look theatrical, especially when several dancers perform together, but its origin is devotional. The dhunuchi is used during worship, and the dance becomes an offering to Maa Durga. The gathering crowd, the rising smoke and the accelerating dhaak make it one of the most recognisable sensory moments of Puja.

What does “Dhaker Tale” mean?

Dhaker tale means “to the rhythm of the dhaak.” The phrase matters because Dhunuchi Naach is inseparable from the drumbeat that guides its pace and energy. At BSS, the official programme uses the combined label Dhaker Tale & Dhunuchi Competition.

Can visitors watch or participate?

Visitors can plan to attend the 7:30 PM programme and watch respectfully. However, the official BSS schedule does not publish competition registration, eligibility or participation instructions. Because a lit dhunuchi involves heat and smoke, do not handle one without direct guidance from the organisers.
