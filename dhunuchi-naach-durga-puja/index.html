DURGA PUJA TRADITIONS

When the dhaak gets louder and the smoke begins to rise.

If you have ever watched Dhunuchi Naach during Durga Puja, you know it is difficult to stand still. It is one of those moments when worship, music and celebration become the same thing.

What is Dhunuchi Naach?

Dhunuchi Naach is a devotional dance performed during Durga Puja, usually around aarti. A dhunuchi is a traditional incense burner, commonly made of earthenware. Burning coconut husk, camphor and incense create the thick fragrant smoke while dancers move to the rhythm of the dhaak.

It is not a performance first. It is an offering.

The sight can be spectacular, especially when experienced for the first time, but the tradition comes from worship. The dhunuchi is used during aarti and the dance becomes an expression of devotion to Maa Durga.

And then there is the dhaak. The rhythm builds, people gather around, the pandal fills with smoke and suddenly the entire space feels different. This is one of the experiences that photographs and videos never quite capture properly.
