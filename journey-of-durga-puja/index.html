The Journey of Durga Puja

Before the dhaak. Before the lights. Before the pandal fills with people, there is anticipation. A feeling that Maa is coming home.

Scroll to begin ↓
🌅
Mahalaya

Jago Tumi Jago

In the darkness before dawn, Bengal wakes to the invocation of the Goddess. Mahalaya heralds Devi Paksha. For generations, the recitation of the Chandi and the story of Mahishasuramardini have made this morning unmistakable: Pujo is coming.

The famous Mahalaya broadcast associated with Birendra Krishna Bhadra is part of this cultural memory. This page uses an original browser-generated ambient soundscape rather than reproducing that copyrighted recording.

👁️
The awakening

Maa comes home

Tradition imagines Durga leaving Kailash for her maternal home with Lakshmi, Saraswati, Ganesha and Kartikeya. The eyes of the idol receive their final touch. The empty pandal begins to feel alive. What was preparation becomes presence.

🪔
Maha Shashthi

Bodhon

The main celebration begins. Through Bodhon, Amantran and Adhibas, the Goddess is ceremonially invoked and welcomed. Curtains lift, faces glow, conch shells sound and the long wait is over.

🌿
Maha Saptami

Nabapatrika

At dawn, the Nabapatrika brings nine sacred plants together as an embodiment of divine energy. After the ritual bath and installation, worship gathers momentum. The pandal is no longer somewhere we visit. For these few days, it becomes home.

🌺
Maha Ashtami

Anjali

Ashtami carries a special intensity. Flowers are held between joined palms. Mantras rise together. Pushpanjali turns hundreds of individual prayers into one collective moment of devotion.

🔥
Sandhi Puja

The sacred juncture

At the transition between Ashtami and Navami comes Sandhi Puja, one of Durga Puja's most powerful observances. Lamps burn, offerings are made and the warrior form of the Goddess comes to the foreground: Shakti confronting destructive force.

🥁
Maha Navami

One last full day

The victory of Durga over Mahishasura lies at the heart of the festival, a story remembered as the triumph of divine power over evil. Yet Navami is also unmistakably human. We know the celebration is nearing its end, so the dhaak feels louder, the adda lasts longer and nobody wants to leave.

🔴
Vijaya Dashami

Farewell, Maa

Dashami brings Baran, Sindoor Khela and the farewell to Durga. The same Goddess welcomed as a daughter is prepared for her journey back to Kailash. Celebration and sadness occupy the same moment.

🌊
Visarjan · Bijoya

Asche bochor abar hobe.

The idol returns to the water, but the Puja does not simply disappear. Bijoya begins with embraces, sweets, pranam and the promise that turns farewell into anticipation.

She leaves. The wait begins again.
