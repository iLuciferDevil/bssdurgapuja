Why is Maha Shashthi important?

Maha Shashthi opens the principal community celebrations of Durga Puja. The welcome to Maa Durga gives the day its emotional character, while worship, shared food and the opening cultural programme bring people together. For someone experiencing Bengali or Sylheti Durga Puja for the first time, Shashthi offers several different ways to participate: morning prayer, an afternoon Bhog visit or the evening welcome.

The day is also a useful introduction to the rhythm of BSS Puja. The opening programme has clearly separated morning, afternoon and evening highlights. You can plan around the part that matters most to you rather than assume you need to spend the entire day at the venue. The Durga Puja meaning guide explains the story of Maa Durga's homecoming in more depth.

Bodhon, Amantran, Adhibas and Agomoni: what do they mean?

Bodhon

Traditionally, the ceremonial awakening or invocation of the Goddess. It signals the beginning of the principal worship.

Amantran

Literally an invitation, expressing the invitation to the Goddess to be present for worship.

Adhibas

A preparatory sanctification associated with welcoming the Goddess for the days of worship.

Agomoni

Songs and cultural expression around Maa Durga's arrival or homecoming. BSS lists Agomoni after the evening observances.

These are explanations of the traditions in general. They do not imply a specific sequence of materials, duration or additional ritual timing at BSS.
