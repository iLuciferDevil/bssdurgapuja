Why BSS is relevant if you are looking for a Sylheti Puja

Bengaluru Sreehatta Sammelani came into existence in 2005 and describes its mission around preserving and celebrating Sylheti culture in Bengaluru. Its first Durgotsav was celebrated in 2019.

BSS's own cultural material highlights traditions such as Dhamail, Kirtan, anjali, bhog, cultural programmes, adda and community togetherness. The aim is not only to perform Puja, but to create a familiar cultural space for Sylhetis and their families in Bengaluru.
