Sylheti and Bengali Durga Puja in Bangalore: the BSS 2026 guide

BSS brings Sylheti community life and Bengali Durga Puja traditions together in Hoodi, with six days of worship, music, performances and community programmes.

Quick answer

Bengaluru Sreehatta Sammelani (BSS) is holding Durga Puja 2026 from 16 to 21 October at The Occasion, Hoodi, Bengaluru 560048. The confirmed programme includes Puja rituals, Pushpanjali, Sandhya Arati, Dhamail, Dhunuchi, Alpona, live music, competitions, Dandiya, Sindhur Khela and community performances.

What is Bengaluru Sreehatta Sammelani?

Bengaluru Sreehatta Sammelani is a socio-cultural association that brings together Sylhetis and friends of the community in Bengaluru. BSS describes itself as a second home for Sylhetis in Bangalore, and Durga Puja is its largest annual gathering.

The BSS identity is visible in the programme rather than treated as a separate theme. Alongside the core Durga Puja observances, the schedule includes Dhamail of Unity, community performances, music and activities for different age groups.

Frequently asked questions

Is BSS a Sylheti or Bengali Durga Puja in Bangalore?

BSS is organised by Bengaluru Sreehatta Sammelani, a socio-cultural association for Sylhetis and friends of the community. Its celebration brings the Sylheti community together within the wider Bengali Durga Puja tradition.

When is BSS Durga Puja 2026?

The BSS programme runs from Friday, 16 October to Wednesday, 21 October 2026.

Where is the BSS Durga Puja venue?

The venue is The Occasion, Sri Devi Anugraha, Anup Layout, Basi Reddy Garden, KR Puram Road, Hoodi, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560048.

When are Dhamail and Dhunuchi scheduled?

The Dhunuchi Competition is scheduled for 7:30 PM on 19 October. Dhamail of Unity is scheduled for 12:00 PM on 20 October.

Are Bhog timings confirmed?

No. Bhog timings are not currently confirmed on the official BSS website and will be added only after the organising team publishes them.
