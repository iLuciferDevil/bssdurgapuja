Durga Puja with the BSS community

Bengaluru Sreehatta Sammelani welcomes you to BSS Durga Puja 2026 at The Occasion, Hoodi, Bengaluru. Founded in 2005, BSS brings together Sylhetis and friends of the community through culture, tradition, food, music and shared celebrations. BSS celebrated its first Durgotsav in 2019.

What to expect

Across the celebration, visitors can experience Puja, cultural performances, Dhamail, Dhunuchi, competitions, live music, food and evening festivities. Check the schedule page for the latest confirmed programme timings.

Frequently asked questions

When is BSS Durga Puja 2026?

From Friday, 16 October to Wednesday, 21 October 2026.

Where is BSS Durga Puja in Bangalore?

At The Occasion, Hoodi, Bengaluru, near Hoodi Metro Station.

Is BSS a Sylheti Durga Puja?

Yes. Bengaluru Sreehatta Sammelani is a socio-cultural association centred around the Sylheti community in Bengaluru, while welcoming visitors and friends from all communities.

Can non-Sylhetis attend?

Yes. Visitors are welcome to experience the Puja, culture, food, music and community celebrations.
