Kali Puja 2026

Our celebrations continue beyond Durga Puja. Kali Puja is another opportunity for the BSS family, friends and neighbours in Bengaluru to come together around a tradition that means a great deal to our community.

Discover the BSS community

Celebrating Maa Kali together

Kali Puja is dedicated to Maa Kali, worshipped as a symbol of strength, protection and the triumph of good over evil. For our community, the occasion is also about reconnecting with familiar faces and welcoming new friends who want to experience the celebration.

Whether you have grown up with these traditions or are visiting for the first time, you are welcome to learn about BSS and join the community.
