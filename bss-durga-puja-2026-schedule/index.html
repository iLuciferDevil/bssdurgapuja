BSS Durga Puja 2026 schedule: Shasthi to Dashami

A chronological day-by-day timetable for rituals and cultural programmes. Bhog timings will be added after confirmation.

Schedule summary

The celebration begins with Maha Shasthi on Friday, 16 October and concludes with Bijoya Dashami on Wednesday, 21 October. Maha Ashtami observances span Sunday and Monday in the programme.
