BSS Durga Puja 2026 schedule: Shasthi to Dashami

Confirmed Puja, Pushpanjali, Bhog and cultural programme timings for 16–21 October 2026 in Hoodi, Bengaluru.

Schedule summary

BSS Durga Puja begins on Friday, 16 October and concludes on Wednesday, 21 October 2026. Bhog is served every Puja day from 1:30 PM to 3:30 PM. The venue is The Occasion, Hoodi, Bengaluru 560048.

Friday · 16 October

Maha Shasthi: opening day

Shasthi Puja begins at 6:06 AM, followed by Pushpanjali at 9:30 AM. Bhog is served from 1:30 PM to 3:30 PM. The evening programme begins at 7:00 PM with Bodhon, Amantran and Adhibas, followed by Agomoni and children's performances.

Saturday · 17 October

Maha Saptami: rituals and live music

Nabo Patrika Sthapan is at 7:30 AM and Saptami Puja is at 8:30 AM. Pushpanjali is at 11:00 AM, followed by Bhog from 1:30 PM to 3:30 PM. Sandhya Arati is at 7:00 PM and the Ochin Pakhi live band is scheduled at 8:00 PM.

Sunday · 18 October

Ashtami programme: Pushpanjali and Dandiya

Ashtami Puja is at 6:00 AM and Pushpanjali is at 11:00 AM. Bhog is served from 1:30 PM to 3:30 PM. The evening includes Karusanskriti at 6:30 PM, Sandhya Arati at 7:00 PM and DJ & Dandiya at 7:30 PM.

Monday · 19 October

Ashtami programme: Sandhi Puja and Dhunuchi

Ashtami Puja is at 6:00 AM and Sandhi Puja is listed at 7:26 AM. Pushpanjali is at 11:00 AM, with Bhog from 1:30 PM to 3:30 PM. Sandhya Arati is at 7:00 PM, followed by Dhaker Tale and the Dhunuchi Competition at 7:30 PM.

For a focused two-day plan, use the BSS Ashtami Puja visitor guide.

Tuesday · 20 October

Maha Navami: Yagya, Dhamail and Dandiya

Navami Puja is at 6:00 AM and Navami Yagya is at 7:30 AM. Pushpanjali is at 11:00 AM, followed by Dhamail of Unity at 12:00 PM and Bhog from 1:30 PM to 3:30 PM. Dandiya is scheduled at 7:30 PM.

Wednesday · 21 October

Bijoya Dashami: Sindhur Khela and Shanti Jol

Dashami Puja begins at 6:00 AM. Sindhur Khela is at 10:30 AM, Bhog is served from 1:30 PM to 3:30 PM, and Shanti Jol is scheduled at 7:00 PM.

Frequently asked questions

What are the BSS Durga Puja 2026 dates?

BSS Durga Puja runs from Friday, 16 October through Wednesday, 21 October 2026.

What time is Pushpanjali?

Pushpanjali is at 9:30 AM on 16 October and at 11:00 AM on 17, 18, 19 and 20 October.

What time is Bhog served?

Bhog is served from 1:30 PM to 3:30 PM every Puja day from 16 to 21 October.

When is Sandhi Puja?

The confirmed BSS programme lists Sandhi Puja at 7:26 AM on Monday, 19 October.

Where is the Puja?

The venue is The Occasion, Hoodi, Bengaluru 560048. The full address and directions link appear above.

Is everyone welcome?

Yes. BSS brings together Sylhetis and friends of the community, and everyone is welcome at the Durga Puja.

Schedule note: This page follows the programme shared by the BSS organising team. It labels 18 and 19 October as two Ashtami programme days. Check this official page or contact BSS before travelling for any later programme change.
