What is Dhamail?

Dhamail is a folk song-and-dance tradition closely associated with the Sylhet region. In its familiar form, participants move together in a circle, singing and keeping rhythm through coordinated clapping. A lead voice may begin the song before others join, allowing the sound and movement to build as a group.

Dhamail is traditionally associated with weddings and other auspicious gatherings. Many songs draw on the stories of Radha and Krishna, and the poet-composer Radharaman Dutta is closely connected with the Dhamail repertoire.

Why Dhamail matters to BSS

Bengaluru Sreehatta Sammelani brings together Sylhetis and friends of the community in Bengaluru. Dhamail gives older generations a familiar cultural connection and lets younger generations experience the tradition as something living rather than something remembered only from home.

It also gives visitors from outside the Sylheti community a direct way to understand what makes the BSS celebration culturally distinct. You do not need to know the language to watch the circle, rhythm and shared movement come together.
