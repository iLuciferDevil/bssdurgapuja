Which Dandiya date should you choose?

Choose 18 October for a fuller evening sequence

The 18 October programme moves from Karusanskriti at 6:30 PM to Sandhya Arati at 7:00 PM and DJ & Dandiya at 7:30 PM. Earlier that day, Ashtami Puja is listed at 6:00 AM, Pushpanjali at 11:00 AM and Bhog from 1:30 PM to 3:30 PM.

See the Ashtami Puja visitor guide for the complete confirmed day plan.

Choose 20 October for Navami and Sylheti culture

The 20 October programme begins with Navami Puja at 6:00 AM and Navami Yagya at 7:30 AM. Pushpanjali is at 11:00 AM, Dhamail of Unity at 12:00 PM, Bhog from 1:30 PM to 3:30 PM and Dandiya at 7:30 PM.

See the Navami visitor guide and Dhamail tradition guide.

Schedule wording: BSS’s published programme labels both 18 and 19 October as Ashtami programme days. This page preserves the organising team’s wording and does not substitute a different ritual calendar.

Frequently asked questions

When is Dandiya at BSS Durga Puja 2026?

DJ & Dandiya is scheduled at 7:30 PM on Sunday, 18 October 2026. A second Dandiya programme is scheduled at 7:30 PM on Tuesday, 20 October 2026.

Where is the BSS Dandiya programme near Whitefield?

Both programmes are at The Occasion, Sri Devi Anugraha, Anup Layout, Basi Reddy Garden, KR Puram Road, Hoodi, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560048.

Which evening has DJ & Dandiya?

The 18 October programme is specifically listed as DJ & Dandiya. The 20 October programme is listed as Dandiya.

Are BSS Dandiya tickets or registration required?

BSS has not published ticket or advance-registration details on the official website. Contact the organisers for the latest confirmed answer before travelling.

Does BSS provide Dandiya sticks?

Dandiya-stick availability is not confirmed on the official BSS website. Bring or purchase arrangements should not be assumed.

Is parking available at The Occasion?

Parking capacity has not been confirmed on the official BSS website. Use the venue and directions section and contact BSS if parking affects your travel plan.

What else is happening on 18 October?

The confirmed programme includes Ashtami Puja at 6:00 AM, Pushpanjali at 11:00 AM, Bhog from 1:30 PM to 3:30 PM, Karusanskriti at 6:30 PM, Sandhya Arati at 7:00 PM and DJ & Dandiya at 7:30 PM.

What else is happening on 20 October?

The confirmed Navami programme includes Puja at 6:00 AM, Yagya at 7:30 AM, Pushpanjali at 11:00 AM, Dhamail of Unity at 12:00 PM, Bhog from 1:30 PM to 3:30 PM and Dandiya at 7:30 PM.
