What to expect

BSS is built around Sylheti culture, community and togetherness. Its Durga Puja experience combines traditional worship with the things people come back for every year: anjali, bhog, cultural programmes, Dhamail, food, music, adda and time with family and friends.

About BSS

Bengaluru Sreehatta Sammelani, commonly known as BSS, came into existence in 2005 and has grown into a platform for Sylheti culture and community in Bengaluru. The association's first Durgotsav was celebrated in 2019.

The BSS website describes its cultural activities through traditions such as Dhamail and Kirtan and positions the organisation as a second home for Sylhetis in Bangalore.
