A practical BSS visitor guide for people travelling from Hoodi, Whitefield, KR Puram, ITPL, Marathahalli and surrounding areas.

Quick answer

BSS Durga Puja 2026 is being held from 16 to 21 October at The Occasion, Hoodi, Bengaluru 560048. Bhog is served from 1:30 PM to 3:30 PM every Puja day. The programme includes Puja, Pushpanjali, music, Dhamail, Dhunuchi, Dandiya, Sindhur Khela and community performances.

What can visitors expect?

Bengaluru Sreehatta Sammelani is a Sylheti socio-cultural association, but the Durga Puja is open to everyone. You do not have to be Sylheti or Bengali to attend.

Visitors can come for worship, Pushpanjali, Bhog, or the cultural programme. If this will be your first community Puja, the first-time visitor guide explains the experience without assuming prior knowledge.

Confirmed-information note: Current official BSS pages do not state parking capacity, entry fees, metro distance or travel times. Those details are therefore not claimed here. Contact BSS before travelling if any of them will affect your visit.
