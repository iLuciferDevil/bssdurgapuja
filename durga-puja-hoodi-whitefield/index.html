BSS Durga Puja in Hoodi: quick answer

BSS Durga Puja 2026 takes place from 16 to 21 October at The Occasion, Hoodi, Bengaluru 560048. Bhog is served from 1:30 PM to 3:30 PM every Puja day. The celebration includes Puja, Pushpanjali, music, Dhamail, Dhunuchi, Dandiya, Sindhur Khela and community programmes.
