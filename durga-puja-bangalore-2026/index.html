Durga Puja in Bangalore 2026: plan your visit to BSS

Confirmed dates, Hoodi venue, daily Bhog, ritual links and cultural highlights for visitors planning Durga Puja in Bengaluru.

Quick answer

BSS Durga Puja 2026 runs from 16 to 21 October at The Occasion, Hoodi, Bengaluru. Bhog is served every Puja day from 1:30 PM to 3:30 PM. The programme includes Puja and Pushpanjali, live music, Dhamail, Dhunuchi, Dandiya, Sindhur Khela and other confirmed community performances. Everyone is welcome.

What is BSS Durga Puja?

Bengaluru Sreehatta Sammelani is a socio-cultural association bringing together Sylhetis and friends of the community in Bengaluru. BSS Durga Puja combines the familiar rhythm of Puja, Pushpanjali, Bhog and evening programmes with cultural traditions connected to Sylhet.

You do not have to be Bengali or Sylheti to attend. The celebration is open to everyone who wants to join the Puja, experience the culture or spend time with the community. Read the Sylheti Durga Puja guide for more context about BSS and Dhamail.
