Durga Puja in Bangalore 2026: dates, timings, venue & complete guide

Everything you need to plan your BSS Durga Puja visit in Hoodi, Bengaluru.

Quick answer

BSS Durga Puja 2026 runs from 16 to 21 October 2026 at The Occasion, Hoodi, Bengaluru. Bhog is served every Puja day from 1:30 PM to 3:30 PM. The programme also includes daily rituals, Pushpanjali, cultural performances, Dhamail, Dhunuchi and Dandiya.

Who organises the Puja?

Bengaluru Sreehatta Sammelani (BSS) is a socio-cultural association bringing together Sylhetis and friends of the community in Bengaluru. Everyone is welcome at the Puja.

Planning your visit?

BSS Durga Puja 2026 is at The Occasion, Hoodi, Bengaluru, from 16–21 October 2026.
